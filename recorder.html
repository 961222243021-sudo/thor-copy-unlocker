⚡ THOR RECORDER

Record your screen, a window or a tab. Then save the video or copy a frame as an image to paste into GPT.
Your browser will ask what to share and shows its own "sharing" indicator while recording. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the video stays in this tab until you save it.
Tip: pause the video on the moment you want, then click “Copy current frame”, and paste (Ctrl+V) into ChatGPT or any chat. Browsers can't copy a whole video to the clipboard, so for full video use Download and attach the file where video upload is supported.
